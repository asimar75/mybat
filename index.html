Battery Sizer

Simulates a home battery hour by hour against your real solar, consumption and EV charging history, then tells you which size pays back — and which is just expensive.

1 Your energy data

Reads the hourly statistics your Energy dashboard already records. Create a token in Home Assistant under your profile → Security → Long-lived access tokens. The token stays in this browser and is only sent to your Home Assistant.

Sensors

Pre-filled from your Energy dashboard. Separate multiple statistics with commas. Start typing to search all energy statistics.

Columns: timestamp, consumption_kwh (total use including the car) or grid_import_kwh + grid_export_kwh, plus optional solar_kwh and ev_kwh. Any row interval works; rows are summed into hours. Download a template.

A synthetic year: 6 kWp solar at ~45°N, ~5,100 kWh household use, and an EV charged three evenings a week. Use it to learn the tool before connecting your own data.

2 Check your data

The result is only as good as this input. Compare the monthly table with Home Assistant → Energy → month view; the totals should be close. Click any day in the chart, or an example below a warning, to inspect it hour by hour.
