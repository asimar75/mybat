Battery & cost
Lifespan & replacement

Wear = age + cycling. A battery is replaced at 70 % capacity; a bigger one cycles less, so it lasts longer. LFP cells are typically rated 6,000+ cycles. The discount rate makes later savings count for less: use what your money would earn elsewhere minus inflation (0 = no discounting).

1 Your energy data

Reads the hourly statistics your Energy dashboard already records. Create a token in Home Assistant under your profile → Security → Long-lived access tokens. The token stays in this browser and is only sent to your Home Assistant.

Sensors

Pre-filled from your Energy dashboard. Separate multiple statistics with commas. Start typing to search all energy statistics.

Meter exports, e.g. from HomeWizard: select all the files at once, one per meter (grid/P1 meter, solar, EV charger, water heater). Running meter totals or per-interval values, any interval, T1/T2 tariff registers and daylight-saving changes are handled; you confirm what each file is before loading.

Or one combined file in this app's own format: timestamp, consumption_kwh (total use including the car) or grid_import_kwh + grid_export_kwh, plus optional solar_kwh, ev_kwh, water_heater_kwh. Download a template.
